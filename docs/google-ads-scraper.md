# Google Ads Scraper

A Google Ads scraper collects publicly displayed sponsored search results for selected keywords.

[[CoreClaw]](https://www.coreclaw.com/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=L7&utm_term=&utm_id=L7) can help marketing and research teams analyze advertisers, ad copy, landing pages, and sponsored search visibility.

## Available Ad Data

Depending on availability, fields may include:

- Search query
- Ad position
- Advertiser name
- Ad title
- Displayed URL
- Landing page URL
- Ad description
- Sitelinks
- Country
- Language
- Device type

## Common Use Cases

### Competitor Advertising Research

Identify which competitors advertise for important commercial keywords.

### Ad Copy Analysis

Study headlines, descriptions, offers, and calls to action used by advertisers.

### Landing Page Research

Discover landing pages associated with paid search campaigns.

### Market Demand Analysis

Estimate commercial activity around target keywords and industries.
