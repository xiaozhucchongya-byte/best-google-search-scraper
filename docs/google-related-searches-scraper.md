# Google Related Searches Scraper

Google related searches show queries connected to the user's original search term.

A related searches scraper helps SEO teams and marketers discover keyword variations, search intent, and content opportunities.

## Available Data

Depending on availability, fields may include:

- Original query
- Related query
- Result position
- Country
- Language
- Device type

## Common Use Cases

### Long-Tail Keyword Discovery

Find keyword variations related to core products, services, and topics.

### Content Cluster Planning

Group related search terms into topic clusters and content plans.

### Search Intent Research

Understand how users refine or expand their searches.

### Market Research

Identify recurring products, services, industries, and concerns associated with target terms.
