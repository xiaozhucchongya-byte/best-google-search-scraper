# Google People Also Ask Scraper

Google People Also Ask results contain related questions associated with a search query.

These questions are useful for keyword research, content planning, SEO analysis, and AI-powered research tools.

## Available Data

Depending on availability, fields may include:

- Original search query
- Related question
- Answer text
- Source title
- Source URL
- Displayed domain
- Question position

## Common Use Cases

### Keyword Research

Discover related questions and long-tail search topics.

### Content Planning

Build article outlines, FAQs, landing pages, and knowledge-base content.

### Search Intent Analysis

Understand what users want to learn before or after the original query.

### AI Applications

Use structured questions and answers in AI assistants and research workflows.
